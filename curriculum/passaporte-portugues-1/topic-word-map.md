**Source evidence:** `†` marks a word verified in that section’s sample lesson or associated grammar sample. Unmarked words are proposed associations, even when they occur elsewhere in the preview. The audio labels A1, A2, A3 in exercises are track numbers.

## Suggested application structure

Use `course → block → section → vocabulary set` for navigation. Link a word to multiple sections through a many-to-many association. Keep a section’s learning goals and grammar metadata separate from its word records. The JSON includes stable section IDs, word associations, source page references, evidence labels and matches to current database word IDs.

A later implementation could add `courses`, `course_sections`, and `section_words` without duplicating the existing `words` table. Existing topic groups can continue to provide cross-course categories.
